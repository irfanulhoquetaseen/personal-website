I started my BSc in CSE at Daffodil International University in May 2025, and university gives you a lot of topics at once. To stay focused, I wrote down a simple roadmap.

## 1. Strong fundamentals

Programming in C and C++, then **data structures and algorithms**. These come up in every course and every interview.

## 2. Problem solving every week

I'm practising on Codeforces. The aim is consistency, not speed.

## 3. Build real projects

Reading tutorials only goes so far. Projects like DIU Campus Navigator, GRABit and this website teach things no lecture does: debugging, Git, and finishing what you start.

## 4. Move towards AI

My long-term path is **Software Engineer → AI Engineer → AI Software Engineer**. Strong software skills come first; AI builds on top of them.

## 5. Share what I learn

That's what this blog is for. Writing things down is the best way to check that I actually understand them.

> "The expert in anything was once a beginner."
